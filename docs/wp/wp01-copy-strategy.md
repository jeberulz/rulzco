# WP01 Copy Strategy — Own Agentic Interface Design

## Executive diagnosis

The current website is polished but strategically broad. Its primary line,
"AI Product Design & Strategy partner for startups shipping intelligent
products," could describe many studios. "From idea to MVP to launch" further
positions Rulz&Co as a general early-stage product partner. Neither sentence
names the interface problem, the human risk, the design-and-build capability,
or the category Rulz&Co wants to lead.

The strongest opening in the supplied research is not generic AI delivery. It
is the interaction layer between increasingly capable AI systems and the people
expected to rely on them. Models can reason, call tools, and act, but users
still need to know what the system is doing, what its output is based on, when
to intervene, and what will happen before an action is committed.

Rulz&Co should own that problem in plain language.

## What is not working in the current copy

1. **The category is too broad.** "AI product design and strategy" does not
   establish a specialist point of view.
2. **The hero describes a stage, not a problem.** "Idea to MVP to launch" says
   when Rulz&Co helps, but not why the work is different or valuable.
3. **The promise conflicts across pages.** The pivot says design and build;
   the services FAQ currently says "Code, no."
4. **AI is presented as a discipline beside design and strategy.** In the new
   position, agentic behaviour is the design context and the interface is the
   product being shaped.
5. **The strongest point of view is buried.** The editorial already argues
   that agents are not better chatbots, but the commercial site still leads
   with generic agency language.
6. **The voice shifts without purpose.** "I," "we," and "Rulz&Co" appear
   interchangeably, weakening the sense of a deliberate founder-led studio.
7. **The proof is ahead of the portfolio.** The existing work demonstrates
   complex workflows, trust, routing, and product judgment. It should be
   framed as relevant evidence, but not misrepresented as a portfolio of
   production agentic systems.

## Positioning system

### Category

**Agentic interface design and build**

Use "agentic interface design" as the category Rulz&Co wants to own. Use
"interface layer" as an explanatory phrase, not the primary category label.

### Audience

Product and AI leaders at startups and mid-market teams that have a model,
agent, automated workflow, or technical proof but do not yet have an experience
people can confidently use.

### Problem

The AI can work, but the experience around it is still opaque, fragile, or
reduced to a blank chat box. People cannot see what it is doing, verify why it
reached an answer, intervene at the right moment, or trust a consequential
action.

### Promise

Rulz&Co designs and builds the interface where people and AI agents work
together: clear workflows, visible system state, grounded outputs, human
control, and graceful failure.

### Differentiation

- More specialised than a general product-design studio.
- More human-centred than backend AI deployment and integration teams.
- More implementation-minded than a strategy-only consultancy.
- Model-agnostic and focused on the product layer people actually use.
- Deliberately small, senior, and close to the work.

### Proof boundary

Lead with the interaction problems Rulz&Co can credibly demonstrate now:
complex workflow design, trust, verification, routing, real-time nudges,
product strategy, interface systems, and frontend implementation.

Do not imply that every existing client project was agentic. New Labs demos
will become direct proof for citations, confidence, approvals, streaming,
agent visibility, and recovery states.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Category:** We design agentic interfaces.
2. **Human outcome:** People can understand, steer, verify, and trust AI work.
3. **Commercial promise:** We design and build the working product layer.
4. **Audience:** Startups and mid-market product teams moving beyond a technical
   proof or generic chat experience.
5. **Method:** Find the right workflow, design human control, build the
   interface, then ship and refine.
6. **Proof:** Complex workflows and trust-sensitive product work now; focused
   agentic interface demos next.

## Voice rules

- Use **we** for the Rulz&Co offer and point of view.
- Use **I** only in founder biography or to explain the deliberately small,
  founder-led delivery model.
- Prefer concrete verbs: understand, steer, verify, approve, pause, recover,
  ship.
- Prefer "agentic interfaces" over "agentic UX."
- Use "interface layer" when explaining the technical boundary.
- Avoid inflated category-leader claims until portfolio proof supports them.
- Avoid vague agency phrases such as "memorable digital experiences,"
  "AI-native thinking," "clarity and craft," and "something worth building."
- Avoid claiming the intelligence is invisible. Agentic systems need legible
  state, evidence, and control.

## Recommended primary copy

### Homepage hero

**Headline:** We design the interfaces where people and AI agents work together.

**Support:** Rulz&Co turns models, tools, and automated workflows into clear,
trustworthy products people can understand, steer, and use.

### Point of view

Agentic products do not need another chat box. They need an interface people
can understand, steer, and trust.

### One-line studio description

Rulz&Co is an agentic interface design studio. We design and build the product
layer where people understand, steer, verify, and approve AI work.

### Footer

Designing how people work with AI.

## Offer architecture

1. **Agentic Interface Diagnostic** — identify the valuable workflow, map the
   user and system responsibilities, define risk and success, and leave with a
   build recommendation.
2. **Agentic Interface Build** — design and build one working interface on the
   client's AI stack, including real states for waiting, evidence, approval,
   failure, and recovery.
3. **Interface Partner** — ongoing product direction, interface design,
   frontend implementation, instrumentation, and iteration.

Existing published price floors remain unchanged during WP01. Pricing can be
revisited as a separate owner ruling once the offer and proof portfolio are
live.

## Direction after WP01

1. Ship one click-to-source document interface as the lead proof.
2. Reframe Labs around focused agentic interface experiments.
3. Add visible evidence for the interaction states each demo solves.
4. Publish a category point-of-view article that links the commercial promise
   to the working demos.
